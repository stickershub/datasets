## What was imported

- `emojis/static`: 120 PNG files
- `emojis/gif`: 97 GIF files, one byte-identical duplicate removed
- `emojis/source images`: 220 derivative files skipped
- Final catalog: 202 sticker entries / 216 assets / 96 generated posters

No unique artwork was removed. One byte-identical duplicate GIF was collapsed,
and 14 same-name static/GIF pairs are presented as one sticker with two formats.
Several names repeat across subcollections (for example, Pepe Coffee and Peepo
Coffee); those are distinct images and are kept separate.

## Takedown

If you are a rights holder and want an asset removed, open an issue at
https://github.com/stickershub/stickershub/issues with the sticker name and URL.
Because the dataset is a static tree, removals are straightforward.
